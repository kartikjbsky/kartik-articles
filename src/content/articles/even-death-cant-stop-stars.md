Even death can't stop stars.

I am made of the same star stuff, where each atom in my body carries a history older than any story I could tell.

Every element has travelled through a cosmic history of birth, destruction, and transformation.

## We are not separate from the cosmos

The calcium in our bones, the iron in our blood, the oxygen we breathe — none of it began with us.

We inherited it.

Perhaps that is why looking at the night sky can feel strangely intimate. We are not merely looking outward. We are looking at the material history of ourselves.

My fire burns eternal, not because I will remain unchanged, but because matter keeps moving, transforming, becoming something else.

Like the stars.

And perhaps that is the closest thing to immortality the Universe ever promised us.
